# Rules from Zaid

## IRES news carousel: cover headline must be a HOOK
- The cover headline (line1 + line2) must make a Las Vegas homeowner, landlord, renter or buyer stop scrolling and want to swipe. It is not a news label or a plain summary of the report.
- Speak to the reader and their money or decision: a question, a warning, a surprise or a "what this means for you". Examples:
  - Weak: "APARTMENT RENTS / FALL"  ->  Hook: "IS YOUR RENT / TOO HIGH NOW?"
  - Weak: "HOME SALES / DROP 22.7%"  ->  Hook: "BUYERS ARE / WALKING AWAY"
  - Weak: "NEW UNITS / OPEN"  ->  Hook: "3,350 NEW / RIVALS FOR YOU"
- Keep the sizes: line1 about 16 characters or fewer, line2 about 12 or fewer. Put the number or punch in red line2.
- Inner slide headings (white + red lines) should also hook, not just label.
- Every hook must still be true to the source. No clickbait the slides can't back up.
- When offering the 3 topic options, each cover idea must already be a hook.

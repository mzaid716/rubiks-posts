# Rules from Zaid

## IRES news carousel: cover headline = news headline WITH a hook
- The cover must read like a real news headline (what happened, in Las Vegas) AND have a hook that makes homeowners, landlords, renters or buyers want to swipe.
- Not a dull label ("APARTMENT RENTS / FALL"). Not an ad or a question to the reader ("IS YOUR RENT / TOO HIGH NOW?"). It is news first, punchy second.
- Hook tools: a surprising number, a clear winner or loser, a contrast, a "first time since", a strong news verb (slide, slash, surge, stall, flip).
  - Weak: "APARTMENT RENTS / FALL"  ->  Better: "VEGAS LANDLORDS / CUT RENTS" or "RENTS SLIDE AS / 3,350 UNITS OPEN"
  - Weak: "HOME SALES / DROP 22.7%"  ->  Better: "VEGAS BUYERS / PULL BACK 22.7%"
- Keep the sizes: line1 about 16 characters or fewer, line2 about 12 or fewer. The number or punch goes in red line2.
- Inner slide headings follow the same style: newsy and punchy, not plain labels.
- Every headline must be true to the source. No clickbait the slides can't back up.
- The 3 topic options must already use cover ideas in this style.

## IRES news carousel: pick EXCITING topics (Zaid, 2026-11-10)
- Choose news people would stop scrolling for. Think of what local news pages like @sincitylvlocals post: new rules and laws, big money deals, records, "first time since", fights and reversals, deadlines owners must act on.
- Skip dull data-only updates (a rent index moving a little, a mortgage rate tick, a monthly sales recap) unless the number is shocking or a record. Use them only as a last-resort filler.
- National real estate news is fine if it clearly applies to Nevada or Las Vegas owners, landlords, renters or buyers (e.g. federal rules, tax changes, mortgage or insurance shakeups, big national rankings that name Las Vegas). Say on the slides how it hits Nevada.
- Fresh follow-ups to a story we already posted are OK if they add a real new fact (a date, a deadline, a number). Say what's new.
- Still check every number against a second source or the original report. Exciting never means made up.

## IRES news carousel: how to FIND exciting topics (Zaid, 2026-11-10)
- This overrides the routine's "fill gaps with a market update" line. Only fall back to a data recap after the searches below come up empty, and say so to Zaid.
- Search wider than the Review-Journal (it blocks fetches anyway). Run these in one batch:
  - Local TV and news: Fox5 (fox5vegas.com), KTNV, News 3 (news3lv.com), 8 News Now, Nevada Current, Las Vegas Sun, Nevada Independent.
  - Government: Clark County Commission and Las Vegas / Henderson / North Las Vegas city council votes (STR rules, zoning, fees, rent help, new projects).
  - National, if it hits Nevada: FHFA, Fannie/Freddie, HUD, CFPB, IRS or tax changes, NAR rules, insurance shakeups, and national rankings that name Las Vegas.
- Search terms that find good stories: "Clark County approves", "commissioners", "new law Nevada", "lawsuit", "deadline", "record", "first time since", "biggest", "$ million", "Airbnb", "HOA", "eviction", "landlord", "tenant", "property tax", "insurance".
- Score each candidate before picking. A topic is good if it has at least 2 of: a deadline or date owners must act on, a rule or law change, big money ($ millions), a record or "first time since", a fight or reversal, a surprise number. A rent index or a sales recap alone scores 0.
- Before sending the 3 options, ask: "Would @sincitylvlocals post this?" If not, replace it.
